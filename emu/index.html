27 keys · bells · tap FX for the list, or turn on FX LOCK to play effects over the picture · tap to play once, hold a key to keep it on or stop it · SELECT changes banks

Change the worldTurn PRESETS for the fifteen worlds. Turn SELECT to move through seven pages of knobs; each knob explains itself as you turn it. Press GLO for Global: turn SELECT to choose a setting and Knob 1 to change it.
Force eventsEDIT is an omen, SEQ picks a random world and REC holds. ARP is an upheaval. Hold LFO for the swell and let go to collapse it. A tap of FX opens the effects. On the real FM-1, hold FX and press keys to play effects while the picture stays on screen; here, FX LOCK does the holding.
Keep what you likeSAVE opens the save menu: Knob 1 picks one of four slots, and SAVE again saves there. Tap a lit key (F, G, A or B) to load a slot; Knob 4, three clicks right, erases it. HOME leaves without saving.

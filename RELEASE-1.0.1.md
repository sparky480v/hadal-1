HADAL-1 1.0.1: steadier under heavy playing, clearer key voices.

- Voices you play on the keys are easy to hear now. Every KEY VOICE was measured against the worlds and set to stand
  clearly above them; the quietest (WORLD'S CHOIR, SHADOW SOPRANO, LULLABY OF THE DEAD, SOPRANO BELOW, KEENING and
  others) were lost under the music before.
- With everything turned up, voices latched, effects kept and the keys being played, the FM-1's processor could run
  out of time and break up the sound. HADAL-1 now times itself while it plays. When it gets close to full, the world
  lightens before anything can break: the pads rest and the choir steps back. If that is not enough, the choir rests
  completely, kept effects take turns (two sound at once), a tapped effect replaces the oldest one, let-go voices
  fade faster, and the work is shared better between the FM-1's two cores. Every few milliseconds it also keeps a
  deadline: if time is about to run out, an effect or bell skips a moment instead of the whole sound tearing into a
  loud buzz. Once things calm down, it all comes back by itself.
- Up to 3 effects can be kept on at once now (it was 4): four of the heavy ones could fill the processor by
  themselves. A saved world with 4 kept brings back the first 3.
- Nothing else changed. Works on older and newer FM-1s (both flash chips).

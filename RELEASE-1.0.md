HADAL-1 1.0: generative dark ambient for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Drones, demonic and angelic choirs, bells and distant sounds that keep changing by themselves, across 15 worlds, with
40 full-screen pictures that melt into each other.

- Works on older and newer FM-1s: both flash chips (Puya P25Q80 and UC25HQ80) are accepted.
- 34 drones, 29 choirs (each with its own way of singing), 41 bells, 135 effects.
- Play bells or sing choir voices on the keys; latch up to 4 voices; split bells and voices across the keyboard.
- Keep up to 4 effects playing; ENV lets everything go at once.
- Four save slots; power-on can bring back your saved world or start a new one.
- A 7-page guide the first time it starts, and help text on every control.
- MIDI in and out over USB.
- Best on headphones. Speaker mode (the default) is tuned for the FM-1's small built-in speaker; switch in GLOBAL,
  SOUND MODE.

Install from The Firmware Hub or any FM-1 web installer. The update loader is included, so you can always switch to
another firmware. Ideas for future updates: polystellarflow@gmail.com

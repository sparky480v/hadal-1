# HADAL-1 version 1.1 plan: the RITUAL layer

Sparse, tempo-less percussion. Never a beat. It should feel like a ceremony in the next room.

## What it is
- A new sound layer, RITUAL, with its own level on the LEVELS page.
- Off (0) by default, so 1.0 sounds exactly the same unless someone turns it up.
- Saved with each world like the other levels.

## How it plays
- No tempo and no grid. Hits come at uneven moments.
- Sometimes a slow cluster of 2 or 3 hits, then silence for a while (10 s to over a minute).
- ACTIVITY sets how often: calm worlds almost never, restless worlds more.
- Each world leans toward its own few drums, but can use any of them.
- Hits sit far back: heavy room echo, darkened top end, low in the mix.
- Never on top of each other, and they stay out of the way of key bells.

## The sounds (8 to start, all synthesized, no samples)
1. FRAME DRUM: deep, soft skin hit
2. DISTANT TAIKO: huge low boom, mostly echo
3. TAM-TAM SWELL: a gong that rises out of nothing and fades
4. BONE KNOCK: dry, hollow click
5. WOOD BLOCK (low): dark knock, like a temple door
6. HEARTBEAT: two slow soft thuds
7. RAIN STICK: a short dark rattle
8. CHAIN HIT: a single low metallic strike

## Keys and effects
- Optional later: a ritual hit can also be played from the FX menu.

## Testing (the same care as 1.0)
- Speaker mode at full volume: no crackle (the deep booms are the risk, so they go through the same speaker path).
- Level match against drones, bells and choirs.
- CPU check with everything at full.
- A listening page with clips of all 8 before anything goes in.
- Then flash, checklist, release.
